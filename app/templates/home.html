How a record becomes provable

  1. Issue

    An approved school enters a result or certificate in its workspace.

  2. Anchor

    TrueMark computes the record's fingerprint and writes only that fingerprint to a blockchain. Names and grades stay in the database.

  3. Verify

    A QR code or record ID opens a public page that recomputes the fingerprint and compares it with the anchored one.

What the proof says, and what it doesn't

It shows

  • The record matches the fingerprint anchored when the school issued it.
  • Changing the name, course, grade or date, even by one character, breaks the match.
  • Whether the school has since revoked the record.

It doesn't show

  • That the school entered true information. That is why only schools approved by TrueMark can issue.
  • That the person holding a certificate is the student named on it.
  • Anything about records that were never issued through TrueMark.
